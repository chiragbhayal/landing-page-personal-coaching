Questions before joining

I’m overweight now. Can I still reach the stage one day?

That’s what the three levels are for. Fat loss first, then muscle, then prep when your physique is ready.

I’ve never competed. Can I do contest prep?

Yes. On the counselling call we’ll tell you honestly which show and category make sense.

Do I need to live in Surat?

No. Most members are coached online. Offline training in Surat is there if you’re nearby.

How is my health monitored?

A doctor is in your group from day one and keeps checking your reports and health markers throughout.

Do I need special food or supplements?

No. Your diet is regular Indian home food. Supplements only if they fit your goal and budget.
